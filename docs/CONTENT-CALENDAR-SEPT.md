# @baronesstattoo — September 2026 Content Calendar

Sixteen posts, four per week (Mon / Wed / Fri / Sat), built on five pillars:
the **estate itself** (your unfair advantage — nobody else in DFW has this room),
**healed work** (trust), **artist spotlights** (each links to their booking page),
**process demystification** (kills booking anxiety), and **social proof**.

How to use: run `supabase/seeds/seed-content-calendar-2026-09.sql` in the
Supabase SQL editor → every post appears in **Admin → Marketing** as *drafted*
with its caption and a shot note. Attach the photo (media URL), set the artist
if relevant, flip to *scheduled* — the daily cron publishes IG/FB posts
automatically. Reels must be posted by hand from the app (Meta's API doesn't
take Reels at standard access); their captions are ready to paste.

Swap any date freely — the pillar rotation is what matters, not the exact day.
Artist order (Caroline, Anna, Tyco, Daniel) is a guess from your IG highlights;
reorder to match who has books open. Ale, Katherine, Mikey, Mayra rotate in
October with the same spotlight format.

**Every caption assumes the bio link is `baronesstattoo.com/book?utm_source=instagram`.**

## Operating notes

- **Cadence beats volume.** Sixteen good posts beat forty rushed ones. If a
  week collapses, keep the artist spotlight and the estate Reel; drop the rest.
- **Reels carry reach, the grid carries trust.** New visitors find you through
  the Reels, then judge you by the last nine grid posts — keep those portfolio.
- **Boost, don't cold-start.** After two weeks, take the best-performing Reel
  (probably Sep 3) and boost it: $5–10/day, 10-mile radius on Firewheel, ages
  21–45, optimize for the Pixel's Purchase event (requires
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_META_PIXEL_ID` set in Vercel).
- **Stories daily-ish, zero production:** today's setup, work in progress,
  reshares of client tags, "chair open Thursday" flash slots.
- **Every caption's only link-path is the bio** → keep it pointed at
  `/book?utm_source=instagram` permanently; artist spotlights name their page
  URL in text since IG captions don't hyperlink.
- Klarna/Afterpay stays as a **pinned highlight + Story reshares**, out of the
  grid.
